```
--- paste_id ---
Title: Some Title
Keywords: tag1, tag2
CID: bafk...
Witness: hex...
IPFS: Qm...

<content body>
```
Old pastes (paste_*) may lack CID/Keywords/IPFS fields. The upgrade script inserts IPFS after Witness line.
